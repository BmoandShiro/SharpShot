# SharpShot privacy policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Publisher: BMOandShiro

This is the privacy policy for SharpShot, a Windows screenshot and screen recording app. The current copy lives in this repository so it stays available at:

https://github.com/BmoandShiro/SharpShot/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

## What this app is

SharpShot runs on your PC. It does not require an account. There is no SharpShot login, cloud library, advertising network, or analytics service.

## Data that stays on your device

- Screenshots and recordings are written only to the save folder you choose. SharpShot does not upload them.
- Settings, including hotkeys and theme choices, are stored locally in `%AppData%\SharpShot\settings.json`.
- Optional debug logs may be written next to the app while it is running. Those logs are not sent to us.

Whatever is on your screen can appear in a screenshot or recording you choose to take. Those files remain yours, on your computer, until you share them yourself.

If you enable microphone or system-audio capture for a recording, that audio is written into the recording file on this PC only. SharpShot does not upload microphone or system audio.

## Network access

Steam and Microsoft Store copies of SharpShot do not check GitHub for updates. Those stores update the app. Those copies also do not download other applications for you.

Copies distributed from GitHub may contact GitHub if "Automatically check for updates" is enabled, or if you choose Check Now. That request goes to GitHub's servers (https://api.github.com) so the app can see if a newer public release exists, and may download that release from GitHub. GitHub's privacy policy applies to that connection. SharpShot does not attach an account or a SharpShot user id to that request.

Capturing and recording do not require an internet connection.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not run ads or third-party analytics inside SharpShot.
- We do not operate our own crash-reporting or telemetry service.
- We do not collect your name, email address, or payment information. Purchases on Steam or the Microsoft Store are handled by those stores.

## Other programs

FFmpeg is included so recording works on this PC. It runs locally.

You can link a program you already installed and open it from SharpShot. That includes a more advanced recorder. OBS Studio is one example. SharpShot does not include those programs, and Steam and Microsoft Store copies do not download them. Each linked program is a separate app with its own terms and privacy policy.

## Children

SharpShot is a general-purpose desktop utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Contact and changes

Questions about this policy can be sent as a GitHub issue on https://github.com/BmoandShiro/SharpShot.

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published in this file. The "Last updated" date above will change with it.
